About Me

I’m a 2D/3D Animation and Video Game Development student at CampusFP Atocha. I love creating visual worlds and bringing ideas to life through art. My work includes digital illustration, concept art, 2D animation, pixel art, and interface design.

My Focus

I enjoy turning concepts into characters, environments, and visual experiences that connect with audiences. I’m especially drawn to character and level design, where creativity and storytelling come first.

How I Work

I’m a creative and curious person. I love learning and bringing artistic ideas into animation, illustration, and video games.

More About Me

Outside of my studies, music and dance are a big part of my life—I’ve been singing since I was a child and have over ten years of urban dance experience. I also completed a tattooing course and enjoy both digital and traditional art.
